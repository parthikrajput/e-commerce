# 19 Content Guidelines: Lustra Platform

This document outlines the content standards, copywriting guidelines, character limits, and product naming conventions for the Lustra platform.

---

## 1. Editorial Headings Rules

* **Capitalization Style:** Headings use title case formatting. Prepositions and articles (such as "and", "or", "with", "the") should remain lowercase unless they start the headline:
  * *Refrain from:* "COMPLIMENTARY SHIPPING ON ALL INDIAN ORDERS"
  * *Write instead:* "Complimentary Shipping on all Domestic Orders"
* **Tone:** Brand headings should sound like editorial taglines. Choose evocative, descriptive phrasing rather than generic titles:
  * *Refrain from:* "View Our Custom Rings below"
  * *Write instead:* "Artisanal Custom Rings, Tailored for You"
* **Spacing:** H1-H3 sections must maintain a `1.30` relative line-height to prevent text overlaps on wrapping lines.

---

## 2. Readable Body Paragraphs

* **Readability Rules:** Keep paragraph line lengths between **50 and 75 characters (including spaces)** to make long descriptions easier to read.
* **Line Height:** Set line height to a comfortable `1.6` relative height. Avoid light grey text colors (`Neutral 300` or below) for body copy to maintain strong contrast and readability.
* **Content Formats:** Split long paragraphs into bullet points when presenting technical information, and format jewelry specifications (like metal type, carat weights, and certifications) in clear tables.

---

## 3. Recommended Character Limits

To prevent layout breaks, use layout truncations for variable user and system content:

* **Product Titles:** Maximum **48 characters**. Truncate longer names with trailing ellipses (`...`) on category grid views.
* **Card Snippet Description:** Limit card sub-descriptions to a maximum of **80 characters**.
* **Community Review Headline:** Limit user reviews header copy to **60 characters**.
* **Review Body:** Limit the visible preview of customer reviews to **180 characters**. Add a "Read More" button to expand reviews if needed.
* **Meta Page Title:** Keep page titles to a maximum of **60 characters**.
* **Meta Page Description:** Keep page descriptions to a maximum of **155 characters**.

---

## 4. CTA (Call To Action) Copy Rules

* **Direct Action Phrasing:** CTA buttons should tell the user exactly what action they are taking. Use active verbs and keep labels to **2–4 words**:
  * *Refrain from:* "Click Here" / "Submit"
  * *Write instead:* "Add to Jewellery Box" / "Proceed to Payment".
* **Visual Hierarchy Guidelines:** Place primary buttons next to lower-contrast text link buttons to give users a clear path forward (e.g. place a prominent "Proceed to Checkout" button alongside a subtle "Continue Shopping" text link).

---

## 5. Structured Product Naming Framework

To maintain a consistent catalog structure, all product names must follow a predefined naming convention:

$$\text{Name Formula: [Creative Design Identifier] + [Carat/Metal Specification] + [Gemstone Variant] + [Product Category Type]}$$

### 5.1 Naming Examples:
* **Classic Choice:** *The Celestial 18k Yellow Gold Diamond Solitaire Ring.*
* **Modern Trend:** *The Aura 14k Rose Gold Emerald Cushion-Cut Pendant.*
* **Traditional Design:** *The Heritage 22k Solid Yellow Gold Choker Necklace.*

### 5.2 Naming Restraints:
* Do not include the manufacturer part number (SKU) in product titles.
* Do not include pricing values or promotional tags (like "Sale", "Offer", or "Free shipping") directly in the product title.
* Avoid using complex gemological terminology in page titles. If required, include these parameters in the technical specification panels instead.
* Capitalize metal and cut names (e.g. "Yellow Gold", "Rose Gold", "Solitaire", "Emerald-Cut").
