# 13 Microcopy Guidelines: Lustra E-commerce

This document outlines the brand voice guidelines, microcopy standards, and interactive copy templates for the Lustra platform.

---

## 1. Tone of Voice Principles

To match our premium positioning, all copy should follow five core tone guidelines:

* **Sophisticated & Warm:** The copy should sound like a personal shopper at a luxury boutique. Use inviting language rather than formal or cold corporate phrasing.
  * *Refrain from:* "Select item options to proceed."
  * *Write instead:* "Crafted to complement your unique style. Choose your metal finish."
* **Encouraging & Inspiring:** Help users envision how the jewelry fits into their personal style.
  * *Refrain from:* "Item added to list."
  * *Write instead:* "Saved to your jewelry box ✨. We'll monitor stock updates for you."
* **Reassuring & Trustworthy:** Be clear and transparent throughout the shopping flow, especially during checkout and size selection.
  * *Refrain from:* "Transaction pending."
  * *Write instead:* "Securing your payment safely... Your items are reserved for you."
* **Clear & Direct (Errors):** Clearly explain form issues without using confusing technical error codes.
  **Refrain from:* "Error: Syntax invalid."
  **Write instead:* "Please enter a valid credit card number."

## 2. Copy Templates by Component State

### 2.1 Navigation & Action Buttons
* **Primary Cart CTA:** "Add to Jewellery Box" (Replaces the standard "Add to Cart" to align with our brand identity).
* **Checkout CTA:** "Proceed to Checkout" (Desktop) / "Secure Checkout" (Mobile).
* **AI Custom Builder Action:** "Complete My Design" / "Consult Private Custom Stylist".
* **AR Try-On Button:** "Try On Virtually" / "Activate Live View Camera".

### 2.2 Text Input & Form Helpers
* **Email Input Placeholder:** "Enter your preferred email address" / "Sign up for exclusive invitations".
* **Shipping Address Input:** "Full name of recipient" / "Complimentary delivery destination address".
* **Password Field Hint:** "Must contain at least 8 characters, including a number."

### 2.3 Interactive Search Copy
* **Default Search Input Placeholder:** "Search collections, diamonds, or ask our Style Advisor..."
* **No Search Results State:**
  * *Headline:* "We couldn't find that."
  * *Body:* "Try another keyword, upload a photo, or let AI help you discover similar pieces."
  * *CTA:* "Ask AI Stylist" / "Browse New Arrivals".

### 2.4 Wishlist Interactions
* **Default State (Empty List):**
  * *Headline:* "Your wishlist is waiting ✨"
  * *Body:* "Save the pieces you love and they will always be here when you are ready."
  * *CTA:* "Explore Jewellery".
* **Price Alert Notification:** "Good news! 'The Golden Aura Necklace' has dropped by 10%."

### 2.5 Shopping Cart & Checkout Steps
* **Empty Cart State:**
  * *Headline:* "Your jewelry box is empty"
  * *Body:* "Let's find something that sparkles just for you."
  * *CTA:* "Continue Shopping".
* **Gift Wrap Offer:** "Add complimentary luxury gift packaging and a handwritten note."
* **OTP Mobile Authorization Hook:** "We've sent a 6-digit verification code to secure your account."

### 2.6 AI Assistant Conversations
* **Chat Window Initial Welcome Message:**
  * "Welcome to Lustra. I am your personal AI stylist. Upload an outfit picture, describe a design, or tell me about an occasion you are dressing for."
* **Style Matches Scanning State:**
  * *Title:* "Analyzing your outfit..."
  * *Body:* "Matching colors, style, and jewelry trends."
* **AI Engine Timeout Fallback:**
  * *Title:* "Our stylist needs a moment."
  * *Body:* "Something went wrong while generating recommendations. Please reload."
  * *CTA:* "Try Again".

### 2.7 Checkout Confirmation
* **Success Banner:**
  * *Headline:* "Your order is confirmed 🎉"
  * *Body:* "We have sent your receipt and live tracking link to your email."
  * *CTA:* "Track Order Delivery".
