# Product Vision

Create a premium AI-powered jewellery shopping platform that transforms
the traditional online jewellery buying experience into a personalized,
immersive, and trustworthy journey through AI recommendations, AR
Virtual Try-On, intelligent search, customization, and community-driven
inspiration.

The platform should make discovering, trying, customizing, and
purchasing jewellery effortless while building long-term customer
loyalty through personalization and memorable experiences.

-   Too many products.
-   Difficult to choose.

# Project Success Statement

The platform succeeds when users can effortlessly discover jewellery
that matches their style, confidently evaluate products through rich
visuals and AI assistance, complete purchases with minimal friction, and
return for future purchases because the experience feels personal,
trustworthy, and premium. From the business perspective, success is
reflected in higher conversion rates, increased average order value,
stronger customer retention, and meaningful engagement with AI-powered
discovery features.
