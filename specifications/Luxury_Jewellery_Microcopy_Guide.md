# Luxury Jewellery E-commerce Microcopy Guide

## Premium AI-powered Jewellery Platform (Gen Z)

For a premium AI-powered Jewellery E-commerce Platform targeting Gen Z,
the microcopy should feel warm, luxurious, conversational, encouraging,
and reassuring.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Empty States

## Empty Wishlist

**Headline:** Your wishlist is waiting ✨

**Body:** Save the pieces you love and they'll always be here when
you're ready.

**CTA:** Explore Jewellery

## Empty Cart

**Headline:** Your jewellery box is empty

**Body:** Let's find something that sparkles just for you.

**CTA:** Continue Shopping

## No Search Results

**Headline:** We couldn't find that.

**Body:** Try another keyword, upload a photo, or let AI help you
discover something similar.

**CTA:** Ask AI

## No AI Recommendations Yet

**Headline:** Let's find your perfect match.

**Body:** Tell us your style, upload your outfit, or describe what
you're looking for.

**CTA:** Start with AI

## No Orders

**Headline:** Your first sparkle starts here.

**Body:** Explore our latest collections and make your first purchase.

**CTA:** Shop Now

## No Notifications

**Headline:** You're all caught up.

**Body:** We'll let you know when something exciting happens.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Error Messages

## Network Error

**Title:** Connection lost

**Body:** We couldn't reach our servers. Check your connection and try
again.

**CTA:** Try Again

## Payment Failed

**Title:** Payment couldn't be completed

**Body:** No worries, your items are still reserved. Try another payment
method.

**CTA:** Retry Payment

## Product Out of Stock

**Title:** Currently unavailable

**Body:** This piece is out of stock right now. We'll notify you when
it's back.

**CTA:** Notify Me

## Invalid Coupon

**Title:** Coupon not applied

**Body:** Double-check the code or try another offer.

**CTA:** View Offers

## AI Error

**Title:** Our stylist needs a moment.

**Body:** Something went wrong while creating your recommendations.

**CTA:** Try Again

## Image Upload Error

**Title:** We couldn't read your photo.

**Body:** Try uploading a brighter, clearer image with your outfit fully
visible.

**CTA:** Upload Again

## Camera Error

**Title:** Camera unavailable

**Body:** Allow camera access to try jewellery virtually.

**CTA:** Open Settings
