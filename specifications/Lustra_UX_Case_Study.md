# 1. Overview

## Project Summary

Lustra is a concept for a premium AI-powered jewellery shopping platform
that combines AI recommendations, AR Virtual Try-On, personalization,
and luxury retail principles to create an intuitive shopping experience
for Gen Z.

## Business Goal

Increase product discovery, improve conversion, and strengthen long-term
customer loyalty through personalized shopping.

## User Goal

Help customers discover, compare, customize, visualize, and purchase
jewellery with confidence.

## My Role

-   UX Research
-   Product Strategy
-   Information Architecture
-   User Flows
-   Wireframing
-   Visual Design
-   Prototyping
-   Design System

## Timeline

8 Weeks

## Tools

Figma, FigJam, Photoshop, Illustrator, Notion, Maze, Google Forms

## Team

# 2. Problem Statement

## Existing Market Problems

-   Difficult product discovery
-   Generic recommendations
-   Limited personalization
-   Lack of confidence before purchase
-   Poor mobile experiences

## User Frustrations

-   Too many choices
-   Hard to visualize products
-   Low trust
-   Complicated checkout

## Business Challenges

-   High cart abandonment
-   Low repeat purchases
-   Weak personalization
-   Limited customer engagement

AI reduces decision fatigue through contextual recommendations,
conversational shopping, outfit matching, and personalized collections.

# 17. Portfolio Presentation

Lustra demonstrates an end-to-end product design process, from research
and strategy to interaction design and high-fidelity prototyping. The
project showcases systems thinking, AI-powered experiences,
accessibility, and premium visual craftsmanship while addressing real
user and business problems in luxury e-commerce.
