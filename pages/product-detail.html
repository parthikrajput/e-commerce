Caring for Your Ring

Fine jewellery, when properly maintained, retains its brilliance and structural integrity for generations. Here's how to preserve the eternal luster of your Aura Solitaire Ring:

Home Cleaning

Soak in warm water with a drop of mild dish soap for 15 minutes. Gently brush with a soft-bristle toothbrush. Rinse under running water and pat dry with a lint-free cloth.

Safe Storage

Store your ring in its individual velvet-lined compartment. Diamonds scratch other gemstones and metals when stored together. Avoid humidity and direct sunlight.

Wear Protocol

Apply perfumes, lotions, and hairsprays before wearing your ring. Remove during swimming, gardening, or heavy lifting. Bring in annually for professional inspection.
