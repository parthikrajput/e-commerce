Jewellery Buying Guides

The Art of Fine Selection

Empowering you with detailed insights into precious metals, diamond cuts, gemstone grades, and care guidelines to make informed lifetime coordinates.

Gold Alloys & Platinum DNA

Choosing the correct gold karat is a balance between durability and gold purity. Lustra offers pieces in 18K and 14K gold, alongside pure Platinum 950.

  • 18K Gold (75.0% Purity): Ideal for statement solitaire rings. Possesses a deep, rich yellow tone with high resilience against scratching.
  • 14K Gold (58.5% Purity): Preferred for intricate settings and everyday coordinates due to increased hardness from alloys.
  • Platinum 950 (95.0% Purity): Hypoallergenic, dense, and naturally white. The ultimate coordination match for solitaire diamonds.
Au Gold Karat Alloys

Understanding The 4Cs

Every diamond curated by Lustra undergoes strict GIA, IGI, or SGL certification checks. We focus on the core attributes that define diamond brilliance.

  • Cut: The single most critical factor determining brilliance. We prioritize Excellent and Very Good cut grades.
  • Color: Ranging from D (completely colorless) to Z. Lustra curates diamonds strictly in the premium D to H range.
  • Clarity: Evaluates internal inclusions. We focus on FL (Flawless) down to VS2 (Very Slightly Included) grades.
  • Carat: The physical weight. Our AI match guides budget allocations towards ideal diamond cut qualities before weight upgrades.
C4 Cut • Color • Clarity

Gem Natural Saturation

Precision Sizing Coordinates

Finding the perfect coordinate size avoids unnecessary exchanges and ensures natural comfort.

  • Rings: Measure the inside diameter of a well-fitting ring or print out our printable sizing chart. Sizing during evening hours yields best outcomes.
  • Necklaces: Chokers (14–16 inches) wrap close. Princess collars (18 inches) align with classic necklines. Opera drops extend to 24 inches.
  • Bracelets: Measure wrist circumference at the bone and append 0.5 inches for a relaxed coordinate drape.
Ø Diameter Metrics

Preserving Eternal Luster

Keep your pieces glowing with proper daily handling and minimal home care.

  • Home Cleaning: Soak pieces in warm water mixed with mild soap for 15 minutes. Gently wipe with a soft microfiber cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals.
  • Storage: Keep diamonds in separate velvet slots. Diamonds scratch softer metals like gold and platinum if stored together.
  • Wear Protocol: Always apply lotions, perfumes, and hairsprays before wearing your fine coordinates to prevent dulling films.
Care Luster Preservation

Verifiable Trust Certifications

Lustra guarantees the authenticity, origin, and grading details of every metal and gemstone.

  • BIS Hallmarking (Gold): Every gold design carries the official Government BIS stamp verifying gold purity.
  • GIA / IGI Diamonds: Solitaire diamond logs include a distinct laser inscription matching official GIA/IGI laboratory grading reports.
  • Conflict-Free Sourcing: We strictly follow the Kimberley Process, certifying that our diamonds are sourced from conflict-free regions.
